About

Conversational AI often replaces human thinking—a process known as cognitive offloading. While efficient, this risks eroding our mind.

We propose a shift to Cognitive Augmentation: designing Conversational AI that scaffolds human thinking to foster learning, agency, and long-term personal growth.

Participation

We invite submissions that identify a core problem or propose a solution related to the risks of cognitive offloading in conversational AI.

• Dependency and reduced self-reflection
• Erosion of critical thinking
• Over-automation of emotional reasoning
• Design strategies for long-term growth

We welcome research, design, artistic, and practice-based contributions. Accepted contributions will be presented in short.

Submission
  • Paper or short essay
  • Poster
  • Video
  • Interactive prototype
  • Speculative design
